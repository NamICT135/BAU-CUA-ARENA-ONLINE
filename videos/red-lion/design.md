# Design

A single red ceremonial lion commands three Bầu Cua dice through four closely spaced, percussive bursts of golden energy. The movement is built from rigid cutout bones rather than deforming the full picture: mane and forearms react around fixed pivots while jaw and brows inherit the head motion.

524 × 724 portrait. Source red/white/gold lion is the focal point; preserve facial pixels and palette. Magenta eye glows support the fierce brow; ivory dice contrast with the amber bowl. Dark purple/red lantern background and fixed paws anchor the composition. Effects remain below or beside the face. Camera is static. Typography: none, the web retains its own interface labels.

Palette: background #210b20, ivory #fff0cf, gold #ffd76c, crimson #b71229; pink light sampled from the source. No full-frame exposure flashes, oversized sparks, or particle blanket. Lower atmosphere fills the complete card behind the existing label.
